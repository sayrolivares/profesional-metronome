Privacy

This app collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, analytics, trackers, cookies or ads, and no data is sold or shared, because none is gathered in the first place.

Not for profit

The app is currently a free, not-for-profit project. It earns nothing from you or from your data.

The microphone

The tuner uses your microphone only while it is listening: after you press Start and your browser asks for your permission. The sound is analysed on your device, as it arrives, to measure its pitch. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. The microphone is released when you press Stop, switch to the metronome, leave or hide the page, or after five minutes without a note. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your browser’s site settings.

What stays on your device

Your settings, custom tunings, metronome presets, saved accent patterns and practice log are kept in your browser’s local storage, on your own device. They are never sent anywhere. The tuner’s list of recent notes exists only while the page is open. Clearing this site’s data in your browser removes everything; the reset and delete buttons in the app remove their parts.

No trackers, no ads

The app loads no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts and sets no cookies. Its typefaces and sounds are part of the app itself, so using it doesn’t contact any other company, and the page tells your browser to refuse connections to other sites.
